What's running tonight: A big reading run. Hundreds of millions of tokens of free, open text: stories, simple encyclopedia pages, old public books. We're watching for the moment a model suddenly learns to copy and follow along. Researchers have seen that switch flip in other models. We want to see if and when it happens here.

Sapling itself, which keeps growing. LIVI, my app. And a creature with Sapling's mind in a body, a smart pet where care has real consequences. More reading, more senses, more of its own choices.
