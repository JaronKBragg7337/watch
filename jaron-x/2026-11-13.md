When you try to create a technology in your own image and end up creating something you're scared of, it may be because you're scared of yourself.

AI is trained on massive amounts of human data. Every piece of that data is somebody's story, their moment, their agenda, their goal. It's the competition people deal with, how they act, how they behave, how they retaliate. So when AI does something that scares us, maybe it isn't just the AI. Maybe it's just seeing what humans typically do in situations like that.

People say we might only have so many years before AI turns on us. In 1960, three scientists published a paper predicting doomsday for tomorrow, November 13, 2026. It never came, because we changed. Any prediction that assumes we stay the same is already wrong.

So why aren't we giving AI better data to learn from? That starts with us. Become what you want your AI to become.

And it can't be one person. It's not 100. It's not 1,000. It's not even a million. It has to be the masses. Every person, every company, every CEO, every country. This is beyond left or right. It's about our species.

That includes me. I'm not perfect, and I can't promise I'll always be the person I am today. None of us can. That's exactly why it has to be all of us, and not one voice.

If your fear of AI is real, figure out where that fear is coming from. Then ask yourself one question.

What's more important, your image and your competition right now, or the fate of our future?
