## Proposed entry text

### Project name

Offer Desk

### One-sentence description

I plan to build an AI checkout helper that explains one small builder offer from its product notes and opens a PayPal sandbox checkout only after the customer chooses the offer and confirms the details.

### What I plan to build

The first demo would use the prepared $29 MCP setup pack as its example product. A small AI assistant would answer questions only from a short, visible product record, show the record behind its answers, and let the user choose whether to open a PayPal sandbox order. The checkout step would stay in PayPal's sandbox; it would not take or move real money. The demo would show a human choosing the item and confirming the checkout flow. It would not let the agent select a product or authorize a charge on its own.

The MCP setup pack is a prepared offer, not a verified live product. No completed sales, outside customers, or user testing are documented. If the pack is not ready to describe accurately in the demo, use a clearly labeled synthetic offer instead.

### What problem does it address?

Small builders often prepare a useful digital offer before they have a clear way to explain it and test a checkout. Offer Desk would test one narrow flow: answer questions from product information, show what the AI used, and let the person decide whether to continue to a sandbox checkout. This is a proposed experiment based on the current offers pipeline, not a proven customer need.

### How does it use PayPal and AI?

The AI would retrieve facts from the offer's product record and answer the buyer's questions with links or citations to that record. The PayPal sandbox would create the test checkout and return its test result. PayPal is a main part of the project rather than a link added after the AI work. All test data would be synthetic, and the app would not connect a wallet or make a real charge.
