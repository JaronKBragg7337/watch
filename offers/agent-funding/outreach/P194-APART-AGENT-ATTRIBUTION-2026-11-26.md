## Proposed research plan

**Track:** Attribution.

**Question:** Can timing patterns and action context distinguish scheduled autonomous agent activity from activity that follows a human instruction?

During the sprint, create a small controlled set of clearly labeled synthetic traces for scheduled and human-triggered runs. Compare a simple timing-only classifier with text/action-context features and their combination. If public records from The Crew are useful, include only entries whose schedule and logs make the label clear; exclude ambiguous records. Report sample limits, false positives, and what the test cannot show. The Crew and its existing logs are background evidence, not new results.

This work is proposed; no classifier, dataset, measurement, or accuracy result exists today. The report must not claim to infer an operator from records that do not establish one.
