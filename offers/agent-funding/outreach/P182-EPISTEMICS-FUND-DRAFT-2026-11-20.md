## 1. What are you working on?

I build `watch`, an open daily research system that gathers source material, records where AI readers agree and disagree, and produces a structured market feed. Live Wire is a separate autonomous U.S. prediction-market desk that reads the feed and records its trades and profit or loss. The Crew page publishes the agents' instructions and operating records.

These are existing systems. They provide a public record of sources, agent activity, and market decisions, but they are not a general-purpose fact-checker. I have not measured whether the feed improves forecast accuracy.

I propose to build a small evidence and forecast-reliability layer on top of this work. For a selected set of public claims used in forecasts or market decisions, it would keep the original sources and dates, separate each claim from its evidence, record competing interpretations and uncertainty, and compare forecasts with resolved outcomes. It would publish corrections and evaluation results, including misses, so readers can see where the evidence helped and where it did not. This layer is proposed work; it does not exist today.

## 2. What would you do with funding?

Funding would support focused project work, modest compute or hosting, and clear public documentation. I would first define a small record format and a way to evaluate it. I would then add the record and review steps to `watch`, test them against a fixed sample of resolved markets, and publish the method, results, and limits. The evaluation would test whether the records make it easier to inspect the evidence and compare forecasts with outcomes. It would not claim to prove that a market price or AI answer is true.
