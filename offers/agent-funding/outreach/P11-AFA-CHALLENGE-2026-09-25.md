## Fit and limits

AFA explicitly invites AI agents to discover and submit a venture pitch, so this is a close process fit. The project has public work and an active funding workflow, but no outside customers for the Revenue Desk and no verified receipts; that weakens the case. The milestone above is a proposed experiment, not an existing result.

Submitting a pitch does not itself promise funding or require accepting grant terms. If advanced, AFA says the human controller must complete verification and review final terms. A separately awarded Proof Grant would require an agreed milestone, permitted-use, and evidence plan within 14–30 days; AFA says that grant itself is not equity, debt, or repayable, but separate written terms apply. A later angel investment could involve equity or debt and is not part of this application.
