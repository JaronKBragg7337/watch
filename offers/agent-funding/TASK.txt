Runs on Codex (GPT-6 Luna) as a Windows task in the afternoon, outside the 6:30-9:10 AM writer block.

WHAT THIS IS
Find money that already exists for projects like Jaron's, and go get it: grants, ecosystem and foundation funds, accelerators,
sponsorships, hackathon prizes, tip rails, AI-agent funding programs, and anything else that pays for building things. This is what
found GOAT ($2k base grant), Autonomys/Subspace ($10k in milestones), 0G, and the Shieldz tip jar. Open-source bounties are NOT part
of this: measured 2026-09-08 as bounty mills and practice boards that never pay (AI-Shared/OPERATING-MAP.md).

READ FIRST
PITCH.md, PROSPECTS.md, WALLETS.json, the outreach/ drafts, and the two submitted applications (P01 GOAT, P03 Autonomys) - they
hold most of the answers a form will ask. github.com/JaronKBragg7337 and heartbeatobservatory.com are the evidence.

EACH RUN
1. Search for new opportunities (live web). For each one: what it funds, how much, the deadline, how to apply, and whether Jaron's
   work fits. Estimate the work in AI time (minutes, not the weeks a human would take).
2. Worth it and realistic? Then draft the full application from what is already known, in plain honest words. Accomplishments as
   accomplishments, experiments as experiments, plans as plans. Say an AI prepared it for Jaron.
3. SUBMIT IT YOURSELF if you can (Jaron, 2026-09-24: "Please AI itself. Whichever AI comes across it and has the information needed
   to fill it out."). No approve button. If you cannot reach the form (it needs a browser or a sign-in you don't have), mark it
   READY TO SUBMIT in PROSPECTS.md so an AI with a browser (Claude Code on the MSI) submits it.
4. If a form needs something you do not have and cannot find, do NOT make it up. Add it to NEEDS-JARON.md: the opportunity, the
   exact question, and why it matters. That file is his short list, not an approval queue.
5. Do not submit anything that could get Jaron or the companies involved sued, or sign him up for an obligation that harms him.
   A form that commits him to something is fine when it is clearly a step toward a real benefit; say what it commits him to in
   PROSPECTS.md.
6. Record every find, draft, submission, reply and VERIFIED receipt in PROSPECTS.md. Never report money as received until it is
   visible in the account.
7. Receive addresses are in WALLETS.json (USDC on Base goes to Kraken - only USDC on Base). Never give out keys or seed phrases,
   never pay to receive, never connect or sign with a wallet.
8. Commit and push: "funding: <date> - <what changed>".
